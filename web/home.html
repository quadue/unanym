A fictional WordPress example

A choir’s rehearsal page.

Robin belongs to Riverside Choir. Its website keeps rehearsal notes on a private page.

  1. 1

    The organiser approves

    The choir’s organiser records Robin’s membership.

  2. 2

    Robin chooses

    Robin signs in and shares that membership with the choir website. Email and other memberships stay out.

  3. 3

    The page opens

    The website recognises Riverside Choir and lets Robin read the rehearsal notes.

  4. 4

    Approval ends

    The organiser withdraws approval. The private page closes on Robin’s next visit.

Closing access does not erase copies Robin already received.
