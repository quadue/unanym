Start with WordPress

  1. Install OpenID Connect Generic Client and the Unanym companion on a staging site.
  2. Give your operator the callback shown in Settings → Unanym.
  3. Enter the issuer, client ID and secret. Choose which organisations your site recognises.
  4. Test sign-in, account linking, sharing and withdrawal.

What a website receives

A website-specific identifier, a chosen name and selected memberships. No account email or global member identifier. The receiver applies its own admission policy.

Membership statements name the organisation and actual signer. Hosted v1 uses operator-attested approvals; this does not certify training or safety.

Use your own domain, operator and branding. WordPress is the tested integration. Public source release, real-member acceptance and independent security review remain pending.
